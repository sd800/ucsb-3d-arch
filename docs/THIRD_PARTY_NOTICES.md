## Geographic and architectural references

University of California, Santa Barbara public GIS: https://www.dfss.ucsb.edu/design-construction-services/maps and https://services1.arcgis.com/4TXrdeWh0RyCqPgB/ArcGIS/rest/services/ . The Buildings2026 layer identifies a DFSS extraction dated 3 February 2026. Historical campus-map paths, lawns, parking, water and flora positions are reference mapping, not current-condition measurements. Attribution: UC Santa Barbara. Only relevant public attributes are retained, with editor/user fields excluded from the landscape snapshots.

OpenStreetMap: © OpenStreetMap contributors. https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright . Source map data are available under the Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0: https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/ . Normalized source and derived footprint/outdoor mapping retain this attribution. Contributed heights and widths are not survey measurements.

USGS National Map 3DEP: https://elevation.nationalmap.gov/arcgis/rest/services/3DEPElevation/ImageServer . Sampled bare-earth elevations form the ground, with simplified flattened building pads.

University architecture and ecology references: https://classrooms.ucsb.edu/interactive-learning-pavilion , https://www.campuscalendar.ucsb.edu/storke_tower , https://www.library.ucsb.edu/events-exhibitions/exhibitions/construction-reinvention-ucsb-library-photo-history-1954-2016 , https://www.dfss.ucsb.edu/design-construction-services/major-capital-projects/jeff-and-judy-henley-hall-institute-energy-efficiency and https://ccber.ucsb.edu/restoration-area-campus-lagoon . Reference photographs and architectural drawings are not redistributed.

Chinese translations, exterior components, vegetation forms, ocean styling and the tower/palm/wave icon are independent project work. The icon is not an official university signature and does not imply university endorsement. The navy/gold palette follows UCSB-inspired colors.
